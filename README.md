# Agentic LLMs

This repository contains my ongoing survey and research work on **agentic large language models (LLMs)**.  
The focus of this work is to understand how modern LLMs are evolving from passive sequence predictors into **autonomous, goal-oriented systems** capable of structured reasoning, tool use, memory-driven adaptation, and coordinated action.

This repository serves as a living research space and will be updated as new insights, case studies, and analyses are added.

---

## Overview

Large language models have achieved strong performance in language understanding and generation, yet many real-world tasks require capabilities beyond static inference. These include long-horizon planning, self-evaluation, interaction with external tools, memory persistence, and robustness under feedback.

Recent research addresses these requirements through **agentic architectures**, where LLMs operate within structured cognitive loops involving reasoning control, reflection, memory, and environment interaction. This repository surveys these developments from both **architectural** and **cognitive** perspectives.

---

## Survey Scope

The survey report included in this repository covers the following themes:

- Neural foundations relevant to agentic cognition in transformers
- Agentic architecture components and control loops
- Structured reasoning frameworks such as Chain-of-Thought, Tree-of-Thought, and Graph-of-Thought
- Self-critique, multi-pass reasoning, and inference-time optimization
- Memory systems and long-term adaptation
- Multi-agent collaboration and organizational intelligence
- Stability and reliability challenges in autonomous LLM systems

---

## Frontier Case Studies Covered

The survey analyzes and synthesizes findings from several **frontier academic and industrial systems**, including:

- **DeepAgent** – scalable integration of planning, tool use, and verification within inference
- **DeepSeek-R1** – reinforcement-driven reasoning optimization
- **SPICE** – symbolic planning and inference for cognitive environments
- **WebRL** – web-based interaction and reinforcement learning for agentic behavior
- **SWE-Agent** – agentic LLMs applied to real-world software engineering tasks
- **Baby Dragon Hatchling** – emergent self-evolving behaviors in language models
- **Brain Rot** – cognitive degradation under self-generated or low-quality data loops
- **AutoGen** and **CAMEL** – multi-agent communication and collaboration frameworks
- **AI Scientist** and **Deep Research** – autonomous research and iterative reasoning systems

These case studies are used to ground theoretical discussions in empirical system behavior and deployment-scale observations.
